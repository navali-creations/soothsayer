**Added:** A separate permission for sharing card opening times and in-game areas.

**Opening Times & Areas** is only visible after you choose to share data through **Community Drop Rates**. Existing contributors keep their original permission for card names and counts; opening times and areas stay private until separately agreed to.

- **Your choice:** use the separate toggle in **Settings → Privacy & Telemetry**, or choose **Agree** or **Disagree** in the app banner. Times and areas are shared only if you agree, including available historical openings.
- **Clearer information:** the sharing controls and privacy policy explain the data being shared, random upload IDs, and how to stop future uploads.
- **Account verification:** linking your GGG account verifies future uploads. Earlier unverified contributions stay unverified, and previously verified contributions stay verified and linked to that account after unlinking.
- **Account unlinking:** unlink even while offline. Your sharing choices stay unchanged; if sharing is enabled, future uploads use a fresh ID without your GGG account details. Linking the same account again resumes its verified contributions without counting earlier openings twice.
- **Upload feedback:** when contributions are waiting for their original GGG account, a visible warning explains how to resume uploads. Your card history stays saved on your device.
